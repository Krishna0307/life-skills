## Question 1

### Which points were new to you?
* Over-communication is important because it helps keep everyone updated about delays or changes and avoids confusion. Group chats make it easier for the whole team to stay informed.
* Requirement gathering is not just discussion; writing things down and taking regular feedback helps avoid misunderstandings.
* Blocking distractions using apps like TimeLimit and Freedom is a new and useful idea for me.
* Time tracking with tools like Boosted can help improve productivity and understand how time is being used.
* Food and exercise also matter because eating well and staying active improves focus and energy levels.

## Question 2

### Which area do you think you need to improve on? What are your ideas to make progress in that area?

* **Timeboxing:** I will do 2-hour deep work sessions in the morning, turn off notifications, and avoid distractions.
* **Dedicated workspace:** I will work in a quiet place without distractions.
* **Focus apps:** I will use apps like Freedom, Forest, or TimeLimit to block distracting websites.
* **Mindful breaks:** I will take short breaks for stretching and deep breathing to refresh my mind.
* **Track my time:** I will use apps like Boosted to see how I spend my time and improve productivity.
* **Better work environment:** I will keep my workspace clean, organized, and quiet.
* **Experiment and adjust:** I will try different methods and improve based on what works best for me.
