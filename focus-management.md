# Focus Management

## Question 1
### 1. What is Deep Work

* It involves working without any distractions
* It helps you learn things faster
* It improves the quality of your work
* It allows you to complete tasks in less time
* It builds strong concentration skills
* It helps you achieve better results in studies and work

---

## Question 2
### According to author how to do deep work properly, in a few points?
* Work in distraction-free environment  
* Focus on one task at a time  
* Schedule fixed deep work time  
* Avoid social media and notifications  
* Take regular breaks   
---

## Question 3
### How can you implement the principles in your day to day life?
* Set a daily fixed time for focused study or coding  
* Turn off phone notifications during work  
*  Use techniques like Pomodoro  
*  Avoid multitasking  
*  Practice regularly to build focus habit  
*  Track progress daily
