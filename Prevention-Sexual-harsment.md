# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)

The Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Act helps create a safe and respectful workplace for everyone, regardless of gender. It applies to all workplaces, whether public or private, and focuses on preventing and handling sexual harassment cases.

---

## What is Sexual Harassment?

* Sexual harassment means any unwanted behavior of a sexual nature that affects a person’s dignity. It can happen to anyone, regardless of gender.
---

## How to Prevent Sexual Harassment

* Create a clear POSH policy defining unacceptable behavior and consequences  
* Conduct regular awareness and training programs for employees  
* Encourage safe reporting without fear of retaliation  
* Maintain confidentiality during investigations  
* Regularly review and update workplace policies
